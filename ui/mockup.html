The authors shipped an RO-Crate with their inputs, workflow, environment and the numbers they reported. reCRATE reads that crate and does the rest.

Paper's data → workflow → compare with the paper's numbers. Each claim is checked against a tolerance the authors declared before anything ran. The agent fixes setup problems and explains differences. It never decides a verdict.
